About Our Hotel

The Gayatri Hotel in Berhampur offers a perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and empowerment. Strategically located in the city center, our hotel provides ample parking, budget-friendly rates, and modern amenities.

We take pride in our team, which includes dedicated women staff who are integral to our operations, promoting gender equality and empowerment.

Enjoy our one-stop facilities for dining, bar, stay, and celebrations, all while supporting a socially responsible establishment. Book your stay with us today!

Gajanan: No Onion Garlic Food

Gajanan is our in-house brand offering pure, traditional Indian snacks and sweets — all made **without onion and garlic**. Perfect for religious occasions, satvik meals, or anyone seeking clean, organic food. No chemicals, no shortcuts — just pure taste prepared with love and honesty.

Business Center

Need to attend a meeting or get some urgent work done? Our Business Center is designed for comfort and productivity. With high-speed internet, quiet surroundings, and a professional setting, it's ideal for corporate guests, freelancers, and travelers on the go.

Cloud 7 Lounge & Mini Bar

Unwind at Cloud 7 – our cozy and affordable lounge bar that doesn’t compromise on vibe. From refreshing mocktails to classic drinks, enjoy a relaxed evening in a classy yet budget-friendly setting. We also offer a compact in-room mini bar option for those who prefer privacy.

Gayatri's Fine Dining

Taste the best of Indian and international cuisine at Gayatri's Fine Dining – where great food meets great ambiance. Whether it's a family dinner, casual outing, or a business lunch, our mini conference hall adjacent to the restaurant offers the perfect setup for discussions over a meal.

Affordable Banquet Hall

Planning a birthday, anniversary, or office gathering? Our banquet hall is the perfect choice for intimate to medium-sized events. It offers elegant decor, flexible seating, and all necessary amenities – all at prices that won’t break the bank. Perfect for those who want a stylish celebration without spending a fortune.
